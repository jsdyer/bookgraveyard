A note in the margin

A catalogue is
only the beginning.

Book Graveyard is a place for rediscovery. Its name is not a claim that a book is forgotten, unavailable or out of print. Open Library’s community catalogue can contain gaps, duplicate records and uncertain dates.

Reading access: “Readable copy listed” and “Borrowing listed” are Open Library’s work-level index labels. They do not apply to every edition. Follow the original record to confirm current availability. Borrowing may need an account; an old publication date is not a worldwide public-domain determination.

Privacy: searches and edition lookups go directly to Open Library when you request them. There is no search on page load. Imported JSON is processed in this tab. This app does not upload your shelf; device storage is opt-in. Normal CoolCode visit counting is separate from your searches.
